Together with [Henri Riihimäki](https://www.kth.se/profile/henrir?l=en) and [Jason Smith](https://jasonpsmith.github.io/) I run the [AATRN-Networks seminar](https://sites.google.com/view/aatrn-networks-seminar). This is an online seminar hosted by the [Applied Algebraic Topology Research Network](https://www.aatrn.net/), where we cover applications and theory of how network structure is revealed through topology and algebra.
