I am an algebraic topologist and data scientist with a focus on questions in computational neuroscience and biology. I lead the mathematics group on *[Algebraic Topology and Networks in Biology (AlgTopNetBio)](https://www.mpi-cbg.de/egas-santander/)* at the [Max Planck Institute of Molecular Cell Biology and Genetics](https://www.mpi-cbg.de/) and the [Center for Systems Biology Dresden](https://www.csbdresden.de/).
